Stake amount query:
 1. Go to Remix (https://remix.ethereum.org/)
 2. Upload MasterBaker.abi
 3. Open the MasterBaker.abi in Remix
 4. Go to "Deploy and run transactions" tab
 5. In "Environment" setting, selection "Browser Extension" with Metamask or crypto.com Onchain wallet
 6. In "Deployed Contracts" setting, click "Add Contract" and paste contract address of MasterBaker from address list document
 7. Select  "userInfo" in "Functions" setting
 8. Use the "pool id" listed below and your address to fill "param0" and "param1"
 9. Click "call". The result will show the stake amount. The unit is 1e-18 CRO. 


Stake withdrawal:
 1. Go to Remix (https://remix.ethereum.org/)
 2. Upload MasterBaker.abi
 3. Open the MasterBaker.abi in Remix
 4. Go to "Deploy and run transactions" tab
 5. In "Environment" setting, selection "Browser Extension" with Metamask or crypto.com Onchain wallet
 6. In "Deployed Contracts" setting, click "Add Contract" and paste contract address of MasterBaker from address list document
 7-a. If "pool id" is 0, select "leaveStaking" in "Functions" setting
 8-a. Input "amount" to withdraw stake. The unit is 1e-18 CRO. 
 9-a. Click "Transact", then it is done.
-----
 7-b. If "pool id" isn't 0, select "withdraw" in "Functions" setting
 8-b. Enter the "pool id" listed below and the "amount". The unit is 1e-18 CRO. 
 9-b. Click "Transact", then it is done.
