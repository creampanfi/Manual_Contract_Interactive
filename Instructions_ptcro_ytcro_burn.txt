PT/YTCRO burn:
 1. Go to Remix (https://remix.ethereum.org/)
 2. Upload Factory.abi or Factory_V2.abi (for pt/ytCRO or pt/ytCRO_V2)
 3. Open the Factory.abi or Factory_V2.abi in Remix
 4. Go to "Deploy and run transactions" tab
 5. In "Environment" setting, selection "Browser Extension" with Metamask or crypto.com Onchain wallet
 6. In "Deployed Contracts" setting, click "Add Contract" and paste contract address of Factory or Factory_V2 from address list document
 7. Select "burn" and enter the amount in "Functions" setting. The unit is 1e-18 CRO. 
 8. Press "Transact", then done.
